## Reference study and design judgment

- **Peshwai & Maharashtrian Heritage**: Traditional ornaments such as Kolhapuri Saaj, Wati Mangalsutras, Thushi chokers, and Chandrakor crescents are presented with cultural reverence (*परंपरा · सुवर्णकला · विश्वास*).
- **Restraint & Dignity**: Avoids garish e-commerce popups, false countdown timers, invented discounts, or auto-sliding testimonial carousels.
- **Editorial Typography**: Stately serif headlines (`Playfair Display` / `Cormorant Garamond`) paired with precise humanist micro-copy (`Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Manrope`).
